How it works

From light to living 3D.

One pipeline takes a performance from the capture stage to a dynamic, explorable 3D asset.

  1. 01

    Capture

    A ring of synchronized cameras records the subject from every side at once — frame-accurate, with no gaps, so motion is seen from all angles simultaneously.

  2. 02

    Reconstruct

    AI-driven neural reconstruction fuses the views into dynamic geometry and appearance — temporally consistent, so detail stays stable from frame to frame.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Results ship as 4D assets ready for game engines, XR headsets, film pipelines and the web — view them from any angle, at any moment.

Technology

Not a 3D scan.
A 3D scan through time.

Static scanning freezes a moment. 4D capture keeps the performance — the way cloth moves, faces change and bodies shift weight.

Every angle, at once

Multi-view capture removes the guesswork of single-camera depth. What the audience sees from any viewpoint was actually recorded.

Photoreal detail

Skin, hair, fabric and fine surface detail are reconstructed from real imagery rather than approximated by hand.

Temporally coherent

Our models reason across time as well as space, reducing flicker and keeping identity and detail stable through motion.

Built for production

Outputs are designed to drop into the tools teams already use — from real-time engines to offline VFX and XR experiences.
